## 🧠 Philosophy: The Foundation of Intent
Before we ask *how* to build a system, we must ask *why* it should exist and *what* it fundamentally is. My background in philosophy drives my approach to engineering:
- **First-Principles Thinking:** Breaking down complex architectural problems into their most fundamental truths before writing a single line of code.
- **Ontology of Code:** Treating software not just as instructions, but as living ecosystems that define how users interact with digital reality.
- **Ethics & Impact:** Designing algorithms and interfaces that respect user agency, cognitive load, and long-term sustainability.

## ⚙️ Systems: The Architecture of Scale
Ideas require robust engines to survive in the real world. I engineer high-performance systems with a focus on strict boundaries, memory safety, and seamless orchestration.
- **Performance-Obsessed:** Deeply invested in hardware-accelerated rendering (WebGL/Three.js), multithreading (Web Workers), and zero-allocation render loops.
- **Decoupled Architecture:** Building modular, event-driven communication buses that allow complex subsystems to interact without tight coupling.
- **Resilience:** Designing backend and frontend systems that fail gracefully, manage state deterministically, and scale effortlessly.

## 🎨 Design: The Aesthetic of Function
A system is only as good as its interface. I believe that true design is the invisible bridge between raw computational power and human intuition.
- **Form Follows Function:** Crafting interfaces where the visual hierarchy naturally guides the user's focus and intent.
- **Cinematic Interactions:** Utilizing math-driven procedural animations, smooth scrolling, and custom shaders to create immersive, tactile digital experiences.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Obsessing over the milliseconds—ensuring every hover, click, and transition feels grounded and responsive.
